## Strategic Overview

Canvas From The Sky is the next evolution of the proven Abu Dhabi Balloon at Saadiyat concept. It layers a **programmable 60m LED canvas ring** around the balloon's ground platform, transforming a premium sightseeing ride into a full cultural night destination.

From the gondola at 300m, guests look down on three of the world's most significant new museums — **Louvre Abu Dhabi, Zayed National Museum, and Guggenheim Abu Dhabi** — framed by coastline and skyline. On the ground, 55,575 LED stems paint that same skyline back as living art.

The balloon is no longer just a ride. It is the **aerial connector of Saadiyat's cultural spine**.

A premium sightseeing flight. No LED show. Clean, family-friendly, accessible.

| Phase | Detail | Duration |

| Ascent | Rise to 300m at ~50 m/min | ~6 min |
| Peak view | 360° panorama: Louvre, Zayed NM, Guggenheim, coastline | ~5 min |
| Descent | Return to ground at ~40 m/min | ~7.5 min |
| Deboarding + turnover | | ~6.5 min |
| **Total cycle** | | **~30 min** |

- **Capacity:** ~6 flights × 30 guests = **~180 guests per day** (at 70% load)
- **Operating days:** ~300 days/year (accounts for 15% weather cancellation, maintenance)

### Night LED Show (Sundown – Midnight)

Three themed shows tied to Saadiyat's cultural institutions. Each show is a distinct, repeatable experience — guests are encouraged to return for all three.

**The Three Themes:**

| Theme | Inspiration | Visual Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Theme A — The Universal Collection** | Louvre Abu Dhabi | Ancient civilisations, gold light, arches, global art history |
| **Theme B — The Nation's Story** | Zayed National Museum | UAE heritage, falconry silhouettes, desert palette, pearl-diving |
| **Theme C — The Future Canvas** | Guggenheim Abu Dhabi | Contemporary abstraction, bold colour fields, generative geometry |

**The Night Experience (per ride, ~41 min full cycle):**

| Phase | What Happens | Duration | LED State |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Arrival walkway** | Guests cross the 3m glowing glass walkway to the platform | ~3 min | Themed ambience animation |
| **Boarding cue** | LED field dims gradually over 30 seconds | 30 sec | Fade to dark |
| **Ascent** | Balloon rises to 300m at ~50 m/min | ~6 min | Rising themed animation synced to ascent speed |
| **Peak experience** | Hold at 300m — Abu Dhabi skyline in every direction | 5 min | Gentle ambience visible far below |
| **Descent to show height** | Balloon drops 275m to 25m at ~40 m/min | ~7 min | Transition animation builds anticipation |
| **Main show** | Balloon holds at 25m — LED canvas at full power | ~9 min 20 sec | Full themed 10-minute show (LED resolution optimal at 25m) |
| **Descent finale** | Final 25m descent to ground, show ends at arrival | ~40 sec | Show completes at ground level |
| **Exit ambience** | LEDs hold soft ambience until all guests clear | variable | Soft ambient loop |
| **Turnover** | Next group boards | ~8 min | Ambient holds |
| **Full cycle** | | **~41 min** | |

- **Capacity:** ~8 rides × 30 guests = **~240 guests per night** (at 70% load)
- **Operating nights:** ~300 nights/year
- **Optimal LED viewing height:** 25m (full ring resolution readable from gondola)
- **Max balloon height:** 300m (tether length per technical spec)

## Part 2 — Tiered Ticket Structure & Museum Partnerships

### Ticket Tiers

| Ticket | Includes | Price |
|---|---|---:|
| **Day Flight — Adult** | 1 balloon sightseeing ride, 9am–12pm | AED 195 |
| **Day Flight — Child** (under 12) | 1 balloon sightseeing ride | AED 125 |
| **Night Show — Adult** | 1 themed LED show ride (choose 1 theme) | AED 295 |
| **Night Show — Child** (under 12) | 1 themed LED show ride | AED 175 |
| **Night All-Access Pass** | All 3 themed rides in one evening (save AED 190) | AED 695 |
| **Private Gondola** | Full 30-person exclusive booking, any slot | AED 20,000 |

Day and Night are fully separate products — no cross-combo. The only upsell path at night is single theme → All-Access Pass. This keeps the booking decision simple: guests arriving at night see two choices, not a matrix of bundles.

### Museum Partnership Programme

**Direct Cross-Discount:**
- Balloon ticket holder → **15% off** admission to Louvre Abu Dhabi, Zayed National Museum, or Guggenheim Abu Dhabi
- Museum ticket holder → **15% off** any Canvas From The Sky ticket (day or night)
- Discount is validated digitally via QR on boarding pass / museum ticket

**Saadiyat Cultural Pass (proposed partnership product):**

| Pass | Includes | Indicative Price |
|---|---|---:|
| **Canvas + Louvre** | Night Show (1 ride) + Louvre Abu Dhabi adult entry | AED 450 (vs AED 475 separate) |
| **Canvas + Zayed NM** | Night Show (1 ride) + Zayed NM adult entry | AED 440 |
| **Canvas + Guggenheim** | Night Show (1 ride) + Guggenheim Abu Dhabi adult entry | AED 445 |
| **Night All-Access + Museum** | All 3 themed rides + any 1 museum entry | AED 850 |

**Revenue from partnerships:**
- Commission on museum referrals: ~5–8% of cross-sold tickets
- Museum-sponsored themed show slot: 1 of the 3 nightly themes co-branded annually (sponsorship fee TBD)
- Combined marketing spend sharing reduces individual marketing budgets

**Strategic investor narrative — the Saadiyat ecosystem:**
Saadiyat Island had ~10% YoY visitor growth in 2024 with 74% hotel occupancy. Three major global museums are anchoring the island as the Arab world's premier cultural destination, targeting Abu Dhabi Tourism Strategy 2030's goal of 39.3M annual visitors. Canvas From The Sky becomes the **vertical dimension** of that experience — the attraction that ties the museums together and gives guests a reason to stay from 9am through midnight. No other attraction on the island offers this connective role. This is not a competitor to the museums; it is a force multiplier for all of them.

## Part 3 — Initial Investment (CAPEX)

Two LED field tiers at identical LED density (9-LED, 55,575 nodes, same aerial show quality). Choice is structural durability and serviceability vs. upfront cost.

### High Tier — Aluminum Sealed Block (Option C)

| Component | Cost (AED) |
|---|---:|
| LED field — sealed aluminum block on raised frame | 16,500,000 |
| Balloon (gondola + envelope + winch system) | 8,000,000 |
| Content launch package — 3 themed hero shows + ambient library | 2,800,000 |
| **Total CAPEX** | **27,300,000** |

- Raised 100–300mm above grade, full service plenum below
- Each 600mm block lifts out individually for maintenance
- Structural life: 20–30 years

### Low Tier — LWAC Concrete Tile (Option E2) ★ Recommended

| Component | Cost (AED) |
|---|---:|
| LED field — 60mm LWAC concrete tile, flush on ground | 13,400,000 |
| Balloon (gondola + envelope + winch system) | 8,000,000 |
| Content launch package — 3 themed hero shows + ambient library | 2,800,000 |
| **Total CAPEX** | **24,200,000** |

- Flush to grade, clean aerial reading, no trip-hazard raised deck
- Each 600mm tile: ~37 kg — single-person lift, no crane for service
- Batch-cast on site using locally sourced UAE LECA aggregate
- Structural life: 30–50+ years

### Recurring CAPEX

| Item | Amount (AED) | Frequency |
|---|---:|---|
| Balloon envelope + helium replacement | 2,500,000 | Every 5 years |
| LED tulip head refresh (8% of 55,575 nodes) | ~300,000 | Every 2–3 years |
| LED driver/electronics major overhaul | ~400,000 | Year 7–10 |

## Part 6 — LED Module Life Expectancy

The two-tier investor choice is fundamentally a structural shell decision — the LED electronics inside are the same in both options and are consumable regardless.

### Component Lifespans

| Component | Rated / Structural Life | Practical UAE Service Life | Service Method |
|---|---|---|---|
| LED chips (electrical) | ~50,000 hrs (~27 yrs at 6 hrs/night) | Not the constraint — other layers fail first | — |
| **Tulip diffuser head** | — | **2–3 years** (UV yellowing in direct UAE sun) | Hand-pull from stem, snap new one on. No tools. ~2 min per head. |
| **Stem assembly** | — | **5–8 years** (sand abrasion, UV polymer fatigue) | Unscrew from tile/frame socket. No tile movement. |
| **Driver electronics (per cell)** | ~10 yrs rated | **7–10 years** (thermal cycling, humidity) | Two-person team lifts one tile with suction grips, swap driver, re-seat tile. ~5 min. |
| **Concrete tile (Low Tier E2)** | 30–50+ years structural | No replacement cycle expected | — |
| **Aluminum block (High Tier C)** | 20–30 years structural | No replacement cycle expected | — |

### Key investor message

The most exposed component (the tulip head) costs ~AED 66 to replace and takes 2 minutes per unit. The structural shell — the component the investor is buying — outlives the 10-year business plan horizon. There is no technology obsolescence risk on the structural layer.

### First 10-Year Maintenance Budget (built into spares)

| Year | Expected Work | Budget (AED) |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Commissioning tulip refresh (~3–5% of heads) + early driver swap | 200,000 |
| 3 | First full tulip head refresh cycle (~8% of 55,575 = 4,450 heads) | 295,000 |
| 5–6 | Second tulip refresh + stem inspection + balloon envelope replacement | 395,000 + 2,500,000 (balloon) |
| 7–8 | Driver electronics mid-life overhaul (~5% of 6,175 cells) | 400,000 |
| 10 | Third tulip refresh + balloon replacement | 295,000 + 2,500,000 (balloon) |

### ROI Risk Factors

- Weather shutdowns reduce balloon-linked revenue (15% cancellation already in model)
- Night show content must be strong enough to drive repeat visits and All-Access upsell
- Museum partnership agreements require lead time — year 1 may launch with discount-only, formal pass in year 2
- Load factor depends on marketing and Saadiyat visitor funnel — early activation of museum cross-discount accelerates ramp-up
- Sponsorship is the highest-upside / highest-variance line item — base case assumes AED 2.5M/yr
