# Revised Pitch Plan — Canvas From The Sky

## Objective

This document outlines the required revisions to the original pitch design to align with the updated **Ring Canvas + Ring Gondola Experience** concept.

The goal is to transform the experience from a **passive viewing artwork** into an **active, explorable, non-repeatable storytelling system**.

\---

## 1\. Core Concept Update (CRITICAL)

### Replace Existing Messaging:

* “Full artwork reveal”
* “Entire canvas visible from above”

### With:

* **Segmented discovery**
* **Explorable canvas**
* **Perspective-driven experience**

### New Core Line:

> “The canvas doesn’t just reveal itself as you rise—you reveal it as you move.”

\---

## 2\. Introduce Ring-Based System

### Add New Section:

## The Ring Canvas System

* Outer Diameter: **90–110m**
* Inner Void (Balloon Zone): **40–60m**
* Ring Width: **20–40m**
* Viewing Altitude: **120–225m**

### Description:

The canvas is no longer a full circular field. It is a **ring-shaped LED system** with a central void for the balloon.

This ensures:

* Safety clearance
* Structural feasibility
* Controlled viewing geometry

\---

## 3\. Ring Gondola Integration (NEW KEY FEATURE)

### Add New Section:

## Ring Gondola Experience

* Guests can **walk 360° inside the gondola**
* Movement changes perspective
* Multiple viewing angles per ride

### Key Line:

> Guests are not fixed observers—they are active participants in discovering the artwork.

\---

## 4\. Experience Flow Revision

### Step 1 — Ground

(No major change)

\---

### Step 2 — Ascent (UPDATED)

Replace with:

> As the balloon rises, fragments of the artwork begin forming across the ring.
> Guests move within the gondola, revealing different segments from different angles.

### Step 3 — Peak Experience (UPDATED)

Replace with:

> The artwork is fully active but never fully visible at once.
> Guests explore the narrative by walking around the gondola.
> The canvas flows continuously around the ring.
