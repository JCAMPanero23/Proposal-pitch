Core Concept

Not a reveal.
A discovery.

The canvas is a ring-shaped LED system — an active, perspective-driven artwork with a central void that guests explore by moving. This is not about ascending to see a flat picture below. It is about discovering an artwork that only exists in motion around you.


From the Ring Gondola, guests walk 360° as the artwork continuously flows around the ring. Each step reveals a different segment. Every angle tells a different part of the story. No two positions look the same. No single image captures everything.

"Guests are not fixed observers — they are active participants in discovering the artwork."

The defining principle is simple: segmented discovery over passive reveal, explorable canvas over static field, perspective-driven experience over single-viewpoint spectacle.

The Ring Canvas System

Not a field. A ring.
Engineered for exploration.

The canvas is a ring-shaped LED system with a central void reserved for the balloon tether. This design ensures structural feasibility, safety clearance, and a controlled viewing geometry optimized for discovery at altitude.

Outer Diameter
90 – 110 m

Ring Width (Canvas)
20 – 40 m

Content is mapped radially to align with both the ring canvas geometry and the circular movement of the gondola. This creates a seamless, continuously flowing artwork — no hard edges, no dead zones, no static moment.
