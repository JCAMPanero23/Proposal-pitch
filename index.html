A complete story, every cycle.

Themed Ambience

Arrival & The Glowing Walkway

The experience begins before you board. A 3-metre glass walkway, LED-lit from beneath, leads through the living LED field to the balloon platform. 55,575 stems glow in tonight's theme around you. The show is already running.

Rising Animation

Boarding & Ascent

The LED field dims over 30 seconds as the gondola lifts. A rising animation, synced to the balloon's ascent rate, builds as the ground drops away. Guests watch the ring shrink below them as Abu Dhabi opens up.

Gentle Ambience

300m — Abu Dhabi in Every Direction

At 300 metres, three world-class museums are visible below, framed by the Gulf coastline and the Abu Dhabi skyline. The gondola holds for five minutes. The LED ring glows as a small, perfect disc far beneath — the calm before the show.

Anticipation Build

Descent — The Show Builds Below

The balloon descends 275 metres at 40 metres per minute. As altitude falls, the LED canvas grows in resolution below the gondola — each stem and tulip head becoming distinct. Anticipation builds with every metre.

Full Power · 10-min Show

Main Show — 25m, Full Canvas

Held at 25 metres — the optimal viewing height for the full 60-metre ring. All 55,575 LED nodes at full output. The ten-minute themed show runs at full canvas resolution, the ring filling the field of view below the gondola railing.

Ambient Exit

Finale & Exit

The show completes as the gondola reaches the ground. The LED field holds a soft ambient loop as guests exit through the living sculpture. The next group boards in under eight minutes. Every cycle is self-contained, repeatable, and complete.

LED Canvas · Technology

The canvas behind the show.

A 60-meter ring built from 6,175 identical 600mm tiles, each carrying nine addressable RGBW stems. Designed from the first sketch to be serviced from above — no specialist crew, no crane, no downtime.

Peak Power

Each 600×600mm tile carries nine LED stems, height-adjustable 400–600mm. IP67 driver in a sealed cavity, accessible by suction grips in five minutes. Tulip diffuser heads — the only consumable — hand-pull replaceable in two minutes, no tools. Heat-rated, sand-sealed, built for the UAE climate.
