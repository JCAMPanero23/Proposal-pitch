EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

What / Why a Helium Balloon
The Helium Tethered Balloon Experience is a proven, premium attraction offering guests a serene 10-minute ascent up to approximately 300 meters for an unforgettable 360° view. Successfully launched and operated as The Dubai Balloon at Atlantis, The Palm, it has already demonstrated exceptional demand, flawless safety, and global visibility — becoming one of the UAE’s most photographed tourism icons. The attraction delivers strong commercial performance, minimal land impact, and aligns perfectly with the UAE’s commitment to sustainable, experience-driven tourism.

Why Saadiyat
Saadiyat Island offers the perfect stage for the next evolution of this attraction — transitioning it from a resort-based experience to a cultural landmark. From above, guests will view Louvre Abu Dhabi, the soon-to-open Zayed National Museum, and the future Guggenheim Abu Dhabi, forming a living story of the nation’s creative and cultural journey.

Proposed Investment / Partnership Model
We invite a co-investor to fund the CAPEX and provide the site required for relocation and installation of the existing system, while Hero Experiences Group delivers complete operational management, including logistics, safety, certification, marketing, and staffing.

EXPERIENCE OVERVIEW

The proposed Abu Dhabi Balloon at Saadiyat will be a signature cultural attraction offering guests an elegant ten-minute ascent to approximately 300 metres above sea level, providing a 360° aerial view of Saadiyat Island’s architectural and natural splendour.

From the gondola, guests will enjoy uninterrupted panoramas of Louvre Abu Dhabi, the Zayed National Museum, and the future Guggenheim Abu Dhabi, framed by coastline, mangroves, and skyline.

The experience will operate as a premium, family-friendly, and fully accessible attraction, welcoming guests of all ages.

Experience Specifications:
- Flight duration: ~10 minutes
- 5 flights per hour
- 10-hour operating day
- 12 guests per flight
- ~600 guests per day
- ~216,000 guests annually (approx.)

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

Envelope:
- Total height: 34m (111 ft)
- Diameter: 22.46m (72 ft)
- Volume: 6200 m³
- Nominal lift: 4800 kg
- Helium loss: <70 m³/month
- Pressure: up to 900 Pa

Gondola:
- Composite + aluminum construction
- Diameter: 5.80m
- Floor area: 13 m²
- Weight: 850 kg
- Capacity: 30 passengers + 2 pilots

Tether:
- Diameter: 22mm
- Resistance: 45 tons
- Length: up to 300m

Remote Winch System:
- Dimensions: 4m x 2.2m x 1.5m
- Weight: 6 tons
- Power: 45 kW
- Ascent speed: ~50 m/min
- Descent speed: ~40 m/min
