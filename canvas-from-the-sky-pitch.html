The Idea

What if the ground itself
was the artwork?

A massive LED pixel field surrounds the balloon platform — thousands of low-profile LED stalks resembling illuminated grass. At ground level, visitors walk through a beautiful, gently animated light garden. It's atmospheric and immersive.


But from the gondola, 225 meters up, the entire field resolves into a living painting. Like pointillism in reverse — you have to ascend to see the picture. The balloon becomes the only key to unlock the artwork.

🌿
Step 1 — Ground Level

The Light Garden

Before flight, visitors walk through the LED field. Rippling colors, gentle pulses, organic patterns. Instagrammable in its own right — but it doesn't reveal the full story. The pre-show that sets the mood and builds anticipation.

✦
Step 2 — The Ascent (0–225m)

The Magic Moment

As the balloon rises, abstract lights below begin organizing into recognizable forms. Shapes emerge. The painting assembles. This is where every single rider reaches for their phone. This is the content moment — the transition from chaos to clarity.

🎈
Step 3 — 225m Peak

The Living Canvas

Full artwork revealed at peak altitude. An 8–15 minute animated narrative with immersive audio delivered through the gondola. Commission-based storytelling — Van Gogh-inspired swirls that pulse, desert landscapes that shift, calligraphy that writes itself across the earth.

Current Operations

Built on what we already fly.

Our tethered balloon currently operates 10-minute flights to 225 meters across three daily windows: Sunrise–9 AM, 9 AM–11 AM, and 6 PM–Midnight, weather permitting (grounded at 25+ knots or thunderstorms). The Canvas From The Sky activation layers onto this existing operation — the LED field is ground infrastructure that doesn't affect flight mechanics.


The content opportunity: Today's 10-minute ride includes ascent, dwell, and descent. The animated ground canvas transforms every phase of that journey into a narrative arc — the reveal during ascent, the full artwork at peak, and the dissolve on descent. To deliver the richest storytelling experience, we propose extending dwell time to support 8–15 minutes of animated narrative at altitude. This positions the balloon as a premium content experience rather than a sightseeing ride, justifying higher ticket pricing and repeat visits.


The evening window (6 PM–Midnight) is the hero slot — darkness makes the LED canvas most vivid and photogenic. Sunrise sessions offer a unique dawn-reveal variant. This naturally creates tiered pricing opportunities across time slots.

Rollout Strategy

Two phases. One platform.

The LED field is permanent infrastructure. The content rotates. This creates a launch moment that drives urgency and a long-term model that drives repeat visits.

Phase 1 — Launch (Months 1–6)

"Proud From Above"

Timed to a national moment — UAE National Day, Eid, or a key milestone. The ground canvas renders iconic UAE imagery visible only from altitude.

  • The UAE flag forming from desert sands
  • Falcons in flight across the seven emirates
  • Arabic calligraphy of the national anthem
  • Maximum press & social coverage at launch
Phase 2 — Long-Term (Ongoing)

"Canvas Seasons"

Rotating content keeps the experience fresh and gives visitors a reason to return. Each season is a new artwork, a new story.

  • Masters From the Sky — Van Gogh, Monet reimagined for aerial viewing
  • Stories of the Land — Emirati artists, local narratives
  • Festival editions — Ramadan, F1, Abu Dhabi Art
  • Brand collaborations & sponsored canvases
